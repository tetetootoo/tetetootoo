# Theresa Schantz

Designer & software engineer working across digital products, interfaces, and technology.

I like figuring out how things should look, how they should work, and then building them. My background spans computer science, product engineering at SAP, independent design and development through Half Odd, and building 0→1 products.

Currently building **Wrestling Octopi**, a social media workspace for planning, creating, scheduling, and managing content.

## Selected work

### Wrestling Octopi
**Product Design · Design Engineering · Full-Stack Development**

A 0→1 social media workspace currently in active development toward beta. I design and engineer the product end-to-end, from interaction and visual systems to application architecture, integrations, testing, and deployment.

[View the product](https://www.wrestlingoctopi.com/) · [View case study](https://www.halfodd.com/)

### BrowserBuddy
**Browser Extension · AI · Privacy**

An experiment in fast, private, in-context AI assistance for the browser. Designed around short interactions such as translating, defining, explaining, or simplifying something without leaving the page.

[View repository](https://github.com/tetetootoo/browserbuddy)

### Half a Love Letter
**Interaction Design · Creative Technology · Development**

An anonymous place to leave a thought, confession, or love note for a stranger to find. Built as an experiment in AI-assisted design and the point at which human visual judgment still matters.

[View repository](https://github.com/tetetootoo/half-a-love-letter)

### Trackster
**Product · Full-Stack Development**

A VAT and expense tracker for managing accounting periods, entries, and exports.

[View repository](https://github.com/tetetootoo/trackster)
